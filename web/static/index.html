About

Asset Monitor aggregates public certificate transparency and passive DNS sources, resolves active A/AAAA records, and records new assets in SQLite.

The local server has no authentication. Keep it bound to 127.0.0.1 and never expose it to an untrusted network. Webhook URLs and bot tokens are never persisted.
